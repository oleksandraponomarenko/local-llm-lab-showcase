# Rebuilding the roadmap around the research goal

## Before

An agent produced a linear roadmap that omitted the baseline, target and hierarchy of work. It treated hardware and funding as fixed and narrowed the intended scope. The initial brief contained a baseline and goal in compressed form, but the resulting plan did not carry them forward.

## Review and corrective action

Oleksandra reviewed the plan, found that it failed the acceptance criteria and returned it for rework. She specified a multifunctional system as the target and made resources variables in the plan. She commissioned Codex to independently review the incident and propose corrective and preventive actions. Claude Code then provided a traceable explanation and rebuilt the plan. Oleksandra chose the corrective direction and defined who would rebuild and who would review.

## After

The revised draft starts from the baseline and goal. It adds a tree of work, branches by resource and capability, and conditions for moving between stages. Codex independently reviewed a later draft. The roadmap remains in revision.

The public roadmap lets a reader inspect the structure of the resulting plan and the conditions on its branches.
