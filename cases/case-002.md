# From an incomplete safety handover to independent research

## Before

An early safety task narrowed the question to the current computer. After a correction, the handover carried forward a vocabulary and questions for the owner while the comparison and analysis were still missing. The receiving session treated the task as ready for an owner decision.

## Review and corrective action

Oleksandra asked for the comparison and analysis, returned the preparatory work to the agent, and clarified the executor and approver roles. The lab added rules to preserve useful analysis during corrections and check that the agent's work is complete before a handover becomes ready for an owner decision.

Oleksandra set the scope for a safety framework that would apply across devices and roles. She commissioned two independent studies with a shared structure, followed by cross-review and reconciliation. The agents performed the studies, checked sources and recorded their own corrections and disagreements.

## After

One agent withdrew an overbroad claim about compression and safety after checking the cited study. The reconciliation identified which questions still needed evidence. The roadmap now gives safety research its own workstream and connects access and action permissions to later integrations. The studies and their reconciliation are complete. The roadmap describes policy and integration work as future stages.
