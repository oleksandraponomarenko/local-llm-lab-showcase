# Task and handover

- **Goal** — What outcome does this work support?
- **Definition of done** — What artifact and evidence will establish completion?
- **Receiving agent's work** — What does the executor owe, and what does the owner decide?
- **Constraints** — What may be read, changed, retained or shared?
- **Context** — Which definitions, decisions and dependencies apply?

## Result

Record the delivered artifact, checks performed, observed results, limitations and the next decision or action.
