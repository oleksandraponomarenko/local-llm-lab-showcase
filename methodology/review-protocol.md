# Independent review

Give the reviewer the original objective, acceptance criteria and candidate result. Where independent proposals are requested, each author completes their own proposal before reading the other.

Check whether the result serves the objective, whether the evidence supports each material claim, and whether the attributed roles and links are accurate. Give the author a chance to respond. Record agreement, corrections, remaining disagreements, evidence gaps and decisions requiring owner approval. Keep the underlying reviews available in their agreed form.

For showcase material, also review de-identification, provenance and permissions. Use the [review template](../templates/review-template.md).
