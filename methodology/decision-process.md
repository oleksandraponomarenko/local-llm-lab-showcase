# Decision process

Prepare a decision with a clear question, feasible alternatives, their consequences and the evidence for each. Identify who proposed each option and who has authority to decide. Record the choice and its rationale after the owner confirms it.

Delivery, independent review and owner approval are separate events. Record the scope of approval explicitly. A publication decision names the material and its permitted form.
