# Orchestration

Oleksandra sets the research goal, decides which work to delegate and defines what evidence will support acceptance. The executor carries out the assigned work, records assumptions and checks the result against the goal. The reviewer checks the result independently. Record these roles separately when they are held by different contributors.

A handover states the goal, definition of done, receiving agent's work, constraints and relevant context. Before marking an item ready for the owner, verify that the research, alternatives or artifact needed for the decision actually exist.

Use the [task and handover template](../templates/task-template.md).
